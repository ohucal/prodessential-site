## 2. Information We Collect

**Email addresses you give us.** When you join our email list or request a free download (for example, a free tagged beat or free loop pack), you provide your email address. This is the only information those forms collect. Email signups are processed and stored by Kit (formerly ConvertKit), our email service provider.

**Purchase information.** When you buy a product, checkout takes place on Payhip, our e-commerce and digital delivery platform. Payhip collects the information needed to process your order, including your name, email address, billing details, IP address, and location data. Payment card details are processed by Stripe or PayPal and are never seen or stored by us. From each sale, we receive your name, email address, and order details so we can deliver your files, provide support, and maintain sales records. Payhip's handling of your data is described in Payhip's own Privacy Policy at payhip.com/privacy. For customers in the EU and UK, Payhip acts as a reseller of our digital products and is responsible for collecting and remitting EU and UK VAT, which requires Payhip to retain certain transaction records under applicable tax law.

**Analytics data.** We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the Site (pages viewed, approximate location, device type, how you found us). Google Analytics uses cookies and uses your IP address to estimate your approximate location; it does not store your full IP address. Analytics cookies are only set after you accept them through our cookie notice. You can decline and the Site will work normally.

**Advertising data.** If we run paid advertising campaigns, we may use advertising tools such as the Meta Pixel to measure ad performance and build audiences. These tools use cookies or similar technologies and share data with the advertising platform (such as Meta Platforms, Inc.). Advertising cookies are only set after you accept them through our cookie notice.

**Technical and log data.** The Site is hosted on Netlify, which may automatically log standard technical information such as IP addresses and request data for security and operational purposes. The Site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which involves your browser sending a request (including your IP address) to Google's servers.

**Information you send us directly.** If you email us with a question, custom beat inquiry, or support request, we receive whatever information you include in that message.

## 3. Information Stored on Your Device

The Site uses your browser's local storage to remember things like the contents of your shopping cart, your audio player volume, and your cookie preferences. This information stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is not used to track you.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

- Deliver purchased and free digital products to you
- Send you emails you signed up for, such as new releases, free loops, and offers (you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email)

- Maintain records of beat license agreements, which identify the buyer by name and email
- Understand Site traffic and improve the Site
- Measure and improve advertising, where you have consented to advertising cookies
- Prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations, including tax and accounting requirements

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

## 5. Legal Bases (EEA and UK Visitors)

## 10. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. The Site uses:

- **Strictly necessary storage:** local storage for your cart, player volume, and cookie preferences. This does not require consent and cannot be switched off without breaking Site features.
- **Analytics cookies (Google Analytics):** set only if you accept them via the cookie notice.
- **Advertising cookies (Meta Pixel, if active):** set only if you accept them via the cookie notice.
- **Checkout cookies:** when you proceed to checkout, Payhip sets its own cookies on its checkout pages under Payhip's cookie policy.

You can change your choice at any time by clearing the Site's data in your browser, which will cause the cookie notice to appear again, or by using your browser settings to block or delete cookies.
